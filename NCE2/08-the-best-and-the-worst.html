The Best and the Worst

最好的和最差的

新概念英语第二册 · 第 8 课 · 中英文对照课文

▶ 点读 / 听力练习
  1. Lesson 8第8课
  2. The Best and the Worst最好的和最差的
  3. Why is Joe's garden the most beautiful one in the town?为什么乔的花园是镇上最漂亮的那一个？
  4. Joe Sanders has the most beautiful garden in our town.乔·桑德斯拥有最美丽的花园。在我们镇上。
  5. Nearly everybody enters for几乎每个人都参加
  6. 'The Nicest Garden Competition' each year, but Joe wins every time.“最美丽花园竞赛”但是乔每次都能赢。
  7. Bill Frith's garden is larger than Joe's.比尔·弗里斯的花园比乔的大。
  8. Bill works harder than Joe and grows more flowers and vegetables, but Joe's garden is more interesting.比尔比乔工作更努力。并且能种植出更多的鲜花和蔬菜。不过乔的花园更有趣。
  9. He has made neat paths and has built a wooden bridge over a pool.他修了整洁的小路还在池塘上建了一座木桥。
  10. I like gardens too, but I do not like hard work.我也喜欢花园。但我不喜欢辛苦的工作。
  11. Every year每年
  12. I enter for the garden competition too, and I always win a little prize for the worst garden in the town!我也会参加花园比赛。而且我总能赢得一个小奖品。因为镇上最差的花园！
